7. KEY ARCHITECTURAL & METHODOLOGICAL TRADEOFFS
   - Hierarchical Clustering: Builds a complete merge history once (O(N^2) memory & time).
     Advantage: K does not need to be committed to beforehand. One dendrogram can be
     cut at any height to test multiple K values instantly without retraining.
   - K-Means: Faster (O(k * N * d)), scales to large datasets, but requires fixing K
     upfront and re-running the entire optimization from scratch for every K candidate.
